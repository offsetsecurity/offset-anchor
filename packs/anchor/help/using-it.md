# Using it day to day

An authorisation is a decision made on a day. Continuous monitoring is what
keeps it true on every day after that.

## Every day: the digest

Turn on the **daily digest** in Settings. One email lists what needs
attention: evidence out of date, overdue tasks and findings, policies due for
review, and the risks in the top band. If it is empty, nothing is slipping.

## Once a week: the tasks and findings

Open **Tasks** and **Findings**. Close what is done, and change the date on
anything that has slipped, with a reason. Findings with owners and dates are
your plan of action and milestones (CA-5), and an assessor reads its history.

## When the system changes: the controls

A new component, a new connection, a changed provider? Update **Assets**, check
the boundary in **System & Baseline**, and open the controls it touches. Update
their status, origination and notes, and attach the new proof.

## When something goes wrong: the incident

**Incidents.** What happened, how bad, where it stands. Afterwards, note what
you learned and what you changed, and raise a finding if a control failed.

## Every quarter: evidence and risks

Evidence older than 90 days is flagged. Collect it again, or change what you
are claiming. Look at the top of the risk register and ask whether it still
reflects the system as it is.

## Every quarter: the package

Export the **Executive summary** and the **Gap report**. Compared quarter to
quarter, they show whether the system is getting safer or drifting.

## Before the authorisation expires

**System & Baseline** holds the expiry date. Start re-assessment well before
it: refresh the evidence, re-check the parameters and tailoring, and put the
package together again.
