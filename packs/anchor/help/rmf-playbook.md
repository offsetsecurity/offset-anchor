# SP 800-53 and the Risk Management Framework

SP 800-53 is the catalogue of controls. The Risk Management Framework (SP
800-37) is the process that picks, implements, assesses and authorises them
for one system. This is the path, and where each piece lives here.

Everything below is also in **Get ready**, which tracks how far you have got.

## The seven RMF steps, and where they happen

| RMF step | What you do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare | Name the system, the people, and the boundary | System & Baseline, Users |
| Categorize | Decide the impact level: Low, Moderate or High | System & Baseline |
| Select | Apply the baseline, then tailor it | System & Baseline, Controls |
| Implement | Do the controls, and say who operates each one | Controls: status, origination, notes, parameters |
| Assess | Check they work, and keep the proof | Evidence, Findings |
| Authorize | Hand over the package, record the decision | Reports, System & Baseline |
| Monitor | Keep it true afterwards | Tasks, Findings, Evidence, the daily digest |

## The baselines

| Baseline | Controls | For a system where a loss would be |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 149 | Limited |
| Moderate | 287 | Serious |
| High | 370 | Severe or catastrophic |

Applying one keeps its controls in scope and marks the rest not applicable,
with the reason. Tailor from there: take out what does not fit the system, and
bring in what it needs, writing the rationale each time.

## What a control needs before it counts

- **An owner**, with an email address for reminders.
- **An origination**: system specific, inherited, hybrid or common.
- **Its parameters filled in.** 679 controls leave values for you to set.
- **A status and a statement**: what you actually do, in a few sentences.
- **Proof**, linked and dated.

## Start with the -1 controls

Every family begins with a policy and procedures control: AC-1, AU-1, CP-1 and
so on. One well-written document can cover several families. It is the
cheapest set of gaps to close, and assessors start there.

## The authorisation package, and where it comes from

| Usually asked for | Where it comes from here |
|---|---|
| System security plan (PL-2) | System & Baseline, control statements and parameters, and the Baseline and tailoring report |
| Assessment results | Evidence, and the Evidence register report |
| Plan of action and milestones (CA-5) | Findings and Tasks, and the Gap report |
| Risk picture | Risks, and the Risk register report |
| Where the system stands overall | Executive summary report |

When the authorising official decides, record the issue and expiry dates in
**System & Baseline**, and keep the signed decision in **Evidence**.
