# What each screen is for

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Get ready** | The plan: what to do next, and how far you have got |
| **Golden thread** | Every risk, what treats it, and the proof, joined up. Broken links are what auditors find |
| **Dashboard** | Where the system stands today, by family, in numbers |
| **System & Baseline** | The system, its boundary, its authorising official and authorisation dates, and the baseline you applied |
| **Controls** | All 1,014 controls and enhancements in 20 families: status, owner, origination, parameters, evidence |
| **Evidence** | The proof, with dates and which controls each item supports |
| **Risks** | What could go wrong with the system, scored, owned and treated |
| **Assets** | What the system is made of, and who owns each part |
| **Policies** | Your policies and procedures, with versions and review dates |
| **Tasks** | The work outstanding, with owners and dates |
| **Incidents** | What went wrong, how bad, and where it stands |
| **Findings** | Weaknesses found, and what closed them. Your plan of action and milestones |
| **Reports** | PDFs for the package: executive summary, gap report, baseline and tailoring, risk and evidence registers |
| **Suppliers** | External services the system relies on, and what assurance they gave (SR, supply chain). Has a sample library |
| **Training** | Who was trained, on what, and when it is due again (AT, awareness and training) |
| **Objectives** | What you are aiming at, how it is measured, and whether you got there |
| **Interested parties** | Who has a stake in the system's security and what they need |
| **Audits & reviews** | Control assessments and reviews, planned and held (CA, assessment) |
| **Communications** | What you tell people about security, to whom, how often, and when it is next due |
| **Escalation** | Automatic reminders for anything with a date and an owner's email, one email each, and who is told as it goes overdue |
| **Calendar** | Everything with a date on it, in one list |
| **Help** | Short answers, like this page |
| **Documentation** | The longer guides |
| **Users** | Who can sign in, and what they are allowed to do |
| **Audit trail** | Who did what, when, and from where. Read only. Administrators and auditors |
| **Backups** | Take one, download one, restore one |
| **Settings** | Email, the daily digest, updates, and the HTTPS certificate |

## Two things that surprise people

**Applying a baseline changes 1,014 rows at once.** Controls in the baseline
stay in scope; everything else is marked not applicable, with the reason
recorded. Tailor it afterwards, one control at a time, with your own reasons.

**The guidance is NIST's own wording.** Each control shows the official
statement. Parameters left blank appear as "[Assignment: …]" in reports, so a
report tells you what is still undecided.
