# Quick start

Fifteen minutes. By the end you will know what the product holds, and what to
do tomorrow.

## Sign in

Your administrator gives you a username, a password and the address. The first
person ever to open a new install creates the administrator account.

## Look at Get ready

Eight stages, 26 steps, from setting the product up to an authorisation you
keep true. Each step says what to do, why it matters, and has a button to the
screen where you do it.

Two kinds of step:

- **Checked for you.** The product looks at its own data and turns them green.
- **Ticked by you.** Things no software can see, such as the impact level you
  agreed.

## Describe the system

**System & Baseline.** A name, a short description, the boundary, and the
authorising official. A few lines each is enough to start.

## Apply a baseline

Still in **System & Baseline**, choose Low, Moderate or High. The catalogue is
scoped in one go, and the **Controls** screen now shows what is in.

## Put something in two registers

- **Assets:** open the **Sample library** tab and add what the system is made
  of. There are 82 to choose from.
- **Risks:** the same. There are 56 to react to.

## Work on ten controls

Open **Controls** and pick ten from one family you know, such as AC or CP. For
each: an owner, an origination, the parameters, a status, and a few sentences
in the notes saying what you actually do.

## Attach one piece of proof

**Evidence → Add.** Attach a file, date it, and link it to a control. That is
the loop you will repeat: implement, record, prove.

## Tomorrow

Export the **Baseline and tailoring** report from **Reports**. It shows which
controls the baseline selected, how each was tailored, and the parameters you
have set so far.
